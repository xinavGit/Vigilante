<b>Wiki Description: </b>The Diplomat is an Impostor Power role that can manipulate the meeting. They can choose a player to manipulate, making them vote for the player the player the Diplomat voted for. They can also reveal themselves for political immunity, which also gives the Diplomat the ability to manipulate two players at a time. However, they will leave the game after a certain number of meetings after revealing.

<b>Options: </b>

- Kills Required To Reveal
- Meetings Diplomat Has Before Leaving Post-Reveal
- KCD Increase Post-Reveal
- Vision Post-Reveal
- Can Vent Post-Reveal
