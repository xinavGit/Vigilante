<b>Wiki Description: </b>The Crusader is a Crewmate Protective role that can choose a player to sanctify on their menu. The chosen player will be fortified for a certain amount of time, and the Crusader will kill the first person to interact with them if they are interacted with.

<b>Options: </b>

- Sanctify Cooldown
- Sanctify Duration
- Protects Target From Kill (both players target and attacker will die if false)
