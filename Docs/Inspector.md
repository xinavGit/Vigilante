<b>Wiki Description: </b>The Inspector is a Crewmate Investigative role that can Inspect players during the meeting to see if they are a certain role. If they are correct, they can publish their role for everyone to see.

<b>Options: </b>

- Inspect Uses Per Meeting
- Can Inspect Same Person Again In Same Round
- Can Inspect Same Role Again In Same Round
- Max Publish Uses
- Inpsectable Roles [Crewmate Roles, Non-Crew Roles, All Roles]
